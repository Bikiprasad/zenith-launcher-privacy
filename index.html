1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

At Zenith Launcher, we believe your smartphone's home screen should be a private, distraction-free sanctuary. We operate under a strict on-device first, zero-tracking architecture.

  • Zero Remote Data Collection: We do not collect, store, sell, or monetize your personal information on any remote server.
  • Zero Advertising Trackers: No third-party ad networks, telemetry trackers, or profiling SDKs exist within the application.
  • Zero Cloud Accounts Required: Zenith Launcher is fully functional offline and does not require an account, email address, or registration.

2. Notification Listener Service Disclosure (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE)

Zenith Launcher offers an optional Notification Listener Service (LauncherNotificationService) designed to give users streamlined, distraction-free notification management directly from their home screen.

What Information Is Accessed

When you explicitly grant notification access in Android Settings, the application temporarily observes:

  • The posting application's package name and icon (to show app notification badges).
  • Notification title, preview text, and timestamp.
  • Available notification action buttons (such as "Mark as Read" or "Reply").

Purpose & User Benefits

  • Icon Badges: Displays subtle notification counters on drawer and quickbar app icons.
  • Notification Triage & Zen Shade: Displays notifications in a clean, categorized bottom sheet for quick dismissal without pull-down clutter.
  • Direct Inline Quick Reply: Allows you to answer messages directly via Android's native RemoteInput without switching apps.
Strict On-Device Processing & Zero Remote Storage Guarantee:

Notification data is processed exclusively in volatile local device memory (RAM).

  • Notification texts and previews are NEVER transmitted over the internet to any server.
  • Notification content is NEVER saved to cloud storage or permanent external databases.
  • Notification content is NEVER shared with third parties or advertisers.
  • Notification content is NEVER fed into external AI models (including Gemini).
  • When you dismiss or clear a notification, its temporary in-memory reference is immediately erased.

How to Revoke Notification Access

This feature is entirely optional. You can enable or disable Notification Access at any time:

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps → Special app access (or Device & app notifications).
  3. Select Zenith Launcher and toggle access off.

3. Device Permissions & Contextual Usage

Zenith Launcher requests runtime permissions solely to provide contextual home screen features:

3.1 Installed Applications (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)

Essential for home screen launchers to display, categorize, and launch installed applications in the app drawer. Processed 100% on-device in local memory.

3.2 Contacts (READ_CONTACTS) — Optional & Contextual

Enables 1-tap call and message actions within the Universal Search bar. Contact queries are executed on-device in local memory only during an active search. Contacts are never uploaded or synced to external servers.

3.3 Calendar Events (READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR) — Optional

Powers the agenda widget, daily briefing glance, and calendar bottom sheet using Android's native on-device Calendar Provider.

3.4 Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — Optional

Provides real-time local weather forecasts, precipitation outlooks, and Air Quality Index (AQI). Approximate coordinates are sent securely over HTTPS to weather providers (e.g. Open-Meteo). Location is never tied to any user identity.

3.5 Physical Activity (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) — Optional

Reads step counts from your device's built-in step sensor for the daily fitness glance widget. Steps are stored strictly on-device in local preferences.

3.6 Camera (CAMERA) — Optional

Powers the offline OCR card scanner in the Offline Wallet Vault. Camera frames are processed on-device in real-time by ML Kit and immediately discarded. No photos or video frames are saved or transmitted.

3.7 Biometrics & Fingerprint (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT) — Optional

Secures the Offline Wallet Vault and locked settings. Authentication is delegated directly to Android's hardware Keymaster. Zenith never sees or stores biometric data.

4. Local Storage & Hardware-Backed Encryption

All sensitive items stored in the Offline Wallet Vault (such as card numbers and private notes) are encrypted using hardware-backed AES-256-GCM encryption via EncryptedSharedPreferences, with keys held in the Android Keystore.

7. Data Retention & Full Deletion

  • Encrypted Backups: You can export and restore settings at will via encrypted local JSON files.
